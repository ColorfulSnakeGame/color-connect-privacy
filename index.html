Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

This policy explains what information the iOS game Color Connect: Cozy Yarn stores and how advertising and privacy choices work.

1. Your puzzle progress

The game saves your unlocked levels, drawn yarn paths, completed puzzles, daily challenge results and streaks, tutorial status, hint credits, and sound and haptic settings locally on your device. This lets you resume your puzzles and keep your preferences.

Playing does not require an account. We do not operate a server that receives your puzzle progress.

2. Advertising and third parties

Color Connect uses Google AdMob to provide banner ads, optional rewarded videos for hints, and occasional full-screen ads between levels. Google's Mobile Ads and User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDKs may process data to deliver and measure ads, manage privacy choices, prevent abuse, and maintain security. Depending on your location and settings, this can include IP address, approximate location derived from IP, device identifiers, ad interactions, diagnostics, and consent choices.

Our ad setting: the game requests non-personalized ads only for all three formats. Ads are not targeted based on your previous browsing or app activity. Non-personalized ads can still rely on contextual information and limited identifiers or storage for ad delivery, frequency control, security, and measurement where permitted.

Google and its partners process their data under their own terms. Please see Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information for advertising.

3. Your privacy choices

Where required, the app presents a consent message using Google's UMP. Ads are requested only when the consent system permits them. If privacy options are available in your region, you can review or change your selections in the app under Privacy → Manage ad privacy choices.

Choosing an option in the consent message does not enable personalized ad targeting in Color Connect. Non-personalized ad requests remain the app's policy.

4. Storage and deletion

Local game progress and preferences stay on your device until removed by deleting the app or resetting device data. iCloud device backups may include app data depending on your Apple settings. Advertising data retained by Google or its partners follows their respective policies.

5. Children and families

Color Connect does not include account registration, direct messaging, or social features. Advertising remains subject to applicable regional and age-related requirements. Parents or guardians can contact us with privacy questions.

6. Changes and contact

We may update this notice if the app, advertising configuration, or legal requirements change. For privacy inquiries or requests, email k__larsson@hotmail.com.
